# Our Services

## ADULTS

### Swallowing difficulties (dysphagia)

Assessment and management of swallowing difficulties, including:

- Difficulty swallowing food, fluids or medication
- Coughing or choking during meals
- Changes in swallowing following stroke or neurological conditions

Where clinically indicated, referral for instrumental swallowing assessment (e.g.
videofluoroscopy) can be explored.

### Communication and language

Support for people who experience difficulties understanding spoken / written
language, or expressing themselves verbally / in writing:

- Developing strategies to support everyday communication
- Support for stuttering or fluency disorders, either from birth or acquired later in
  life
- Cognitive challenges such as difficulties with memory, attention, planning and
  problem-solving that affect communication
- Communication changes following stroke, brain injury or other acquired
  neurological conditions

### Neurological Rehabilitation

Individualised communication rehabilitation for people following:

- Stroke / aphasia
- Traumatic brain injury
- Parkinsons disease (LSVT certified)
- Other acquired neurological conditions
- Support for alternative and augmentative communication (AAC)

### Dementia and Communication

Support for people living with dementia and their families, including:

- Maintaining communication and participation
- Strategies to support everyday conversations
- Adapting communication as needs change
- Education and support for family members, caregivers and care homes

## CHILDREN

### Speech Sound Difficulties

Supporting children to develop clearer, more confident speech, including:

- Speech sound development
- Difficulties producing particular sounds or speech sound disorders
- Speech clarity and intelligibility

### Early Communication and Language Development

Supporting preschoolers to develop the foundations for communication, including:

- Early communication skills and interactions
- Developing first words and early vocabulary
- Supporting language development through play and everyday activities
- Parent and caregiver coaching

### Fluency and Stuttering

Assessment and support for children and teenagers (and adults) who stutter,
including:

- Strategies and individualised therapy
- Supporting confidence and positive communication experiences
- Guidance and support for families

### Feeding and Swallowing

Supporting children and families experiencing feeding and swallowing difficulties,
including:

- Fussy or selective eating
- Food refusal and restricted food preferences
- Difficulties with different food textures
- Chewing and oral skills
- Swallowing difficulties (paediatric dysphagia)
- Developing positive and safe mealtime experiences

### Alternative and Augmentative Communication (AAC)

Supporting children who benefit from additional ways to communicate, including:

- Visual supports and communication boards
- Communication books
- Low-tech communication systems
- Supporting functional communication across everyday environments
- Referrals to high-tech AAC services can also be supported
