# Our Team

Alice Milne

Founder and Speech Language Therapist

BA(Hons), MSLP, RMNZSTA

Alice grew up in Western Southland. She returned home to Invercargill in 2019 after
studying and working in New Zealand and Europe. She graduated from the
University of Otago with a Bachelors of Arts (Hons I) in French, and the University of
Canterbury with a Masters of Speech Language Pathology (Distinction).

She has over 7 years of experience working as a Speech Language Therapist in
hospitals and community settings, with both adults and children. She is passionate
about helping people build confidence, develop their skills and participate more fully
in everyday life.

Reaching out for support can often feel daunting, so she aims to create a welcoming,
supportive space where you and your whānau feel listened to, understood and
involved in your therapy journey.
